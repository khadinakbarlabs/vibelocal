# Local competitor comparison

Read [WORKFLOW.md](../vibelocal/references/WORKFLOW.md) and [ACTORS.md](../vibelocal/references/ACTORS.md). Reuse the business context, primary service and area. Ask for missing branch identity or service; do not compare every business in a city.

Use supplied public listings or plan a small `places` query for that service/location, normally 10–20 results. A `local-pack` snapshot can answer who appeared in that Search pack; it is distinct from broad Maps discovery. Select 3–5 genuinely relevant candidates from observed results. Verify category/service, branch location, public listing URL and place ID. Keep ambiguous matches in a review queue, not the benchmark.

Create a source-dated comparison matrix: public name/category, relevant service evidence, website/branch page, observed rating and review count, sampled recurring review themes if approved, and measured visibility only where matching query/settings data exists. Missing data is unknown. Broad Maps discovery order is not automatically a precise grid rank.

Look for actionable differences the owner can verify: an inaccurate category, a service that is offered but not explained, clearer appointment/contact information, useful access information, authentic branch content, unresolved review themes or a measured location-specific visibility gap. Do not infer revenue, ad spend, traffic, backlink strength or Google's factor weights from public profile counts.

Deliver three evidence-backed opportunities with the source, likely user benefit, effort estimate labelled as an estimate and a verification step. Recommend one small next change. Use public business data only; no bulk contact enrichment, outreach or copying competitors' descriptions/images. Writing or sending to competitors is outside this route.
