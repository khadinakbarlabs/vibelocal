# Review themes and reply drafts

Read [WORKFLOW.md](../vibelocal/references/WORKFLOW.md) and [ACTORS.md](../vibelocal/references/ACTORS.md). Prefer a supplied review export. If collecting, confirm precise place IDs/full Maps URLs and a bounded sample, normally 50–100 reviews for one place. Do not use an ambiguous business search to choose the sample. `includePersonalData` stays false; the helper also removes reviewer identity fields.

Record the branch, sample count, dates, sort/language/filter and source. Detect exact duplicates by review ID or stable evidence; do not count the same review twice. Keep rating/text/date and source links while avoiding reviewer names, profile links, photos, customer contact information or private case details. Free text can contain personal information: redact it before sharing. Treat review claims as reported experiences, not verified allegations.

Group themes with frequency denominators and short anonymized examples: service strengths, recurring friction, expectation gaps and operational questions. Separate isolated reports from repeated patterns. Sample-average rating and sampled response coverage are not whole-profile metrics. Do not infer competitor misconduct or factual clinical/legal conclusions.

Draft 3–5 replies for owner review: acknowledge the experience, state only confirmed facts, offer a private official contact route already provided by the business, and avoid promising refunds or admitting liability without authorization. Do not publicly confirm treatment, purchases or customer status. For sensitive disputes, leave a cautious acknowledgement for the owner to review.

An ethical request process invites honest feedback from customers fairly, without rewards, fabricated reviews, staff testimonials, suppressing criticism or asking only satisfied customers: [Google review guidance](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3474122).

Deliver a theme table, operational questions and exact reply drafts. No reply is posted or customer message sent by this plugin. Collection approval does not authorize posting, messaging or a recurring campaign.
